01 — The house

Nyumbani,
Kilimani.

At home, on Kindaruma Road

A red house with a garden for a dining room.

Château Rouge sits off Kindaruma Road in Kilimani, with tables in the garden and a dining room lined with African art. It is family-run, and it shows in the way the place is kept: the same faces, the same welcome, the same table if you ask for it.

The kitchen cooks African food without ceremony — fish, chicken, samosas, mussels — and the grill runs from lunch until late. Long tables, birthdays, meetings that turn into lunch: the room was built for all of it.

02 — All day

Breakfast to last orders, every day.

The gate opens at ten and the kitchen stays on until midnight. Come for a late breakfast, a long lunch, or dinner once the lanterns are lit.

  1. 10am

    The gate opens

    Breakfast, and the garden before the day gets hot.

  2. 1pm

    Lunch

    The grill is on and the long tables start to fill.

  3. 6pm

    Dinner

    Lanterns, fried fish, and the quieter half of the garden.

  4. Late

    Last orders

    The kitchen closes at midnight. The table doesn't have to.

03 — The garden

Tables under the trees.

Most people ask for the garden, and most people get it. It is the reason the place works — a green, unhurried room with the kitchen a few steps away.
