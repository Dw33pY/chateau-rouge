Questions

Where is Château Rouge?

Kindaruma Road, Kilimani, Nairobi. There is parking on the property.

Do you have outdoor seating?

Yes — garden tables under the trees. Ask for one when you book.

Can you host a group or a celebration?

Yes: birthdays, family lunches, weddings and meetings. Call ahead and tell us how many are coming.

Do you do takeaway and delivery?

Yes. Call the kitchen on +254 722 240630 to order.

How much does it cost?

Most people spend around KES 1,300–3,200 a head, depending on what's ordered.

What time do you open?

Every day, from 10am until midnight.
