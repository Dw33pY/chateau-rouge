The board.

African cooking, cooked plainly. The kitchen runs a longer menu than this and it moves with the day — what follows is what the tables order most.

Open daily from 10am Garden tables Kindaruma Road, Kilimani

Ask what came in today

The board changes with the market and the morning's delivery, so this page is a guide rather than a printed card. Call the restaurant and we will tell you what is on, what is good, and what to order for the table. Most people spend somewhere between KES 1,300 and 3,200 a head.

The fish

From the fryer and the fire

  • Whole fried fish

    The dish the table orders first. Served whole, to share — head, tail and all.

  • Chama fish

    Small fish, fried crisp. Best eaten with your fingers and a cold drink.

The chicken

Fried down or over the fire

  • Dry fry chicken

    Chopped and fried down with onion and chilli, the Kenyan way. Order it with a side of chips.

  • Grilled chicken

    Over the fire, for the people who came for the grill. Ask for it well done.

Small plates

To start, or to keep the table busy

  • Samosa

    Crisp pastry, hot filling, gone in three bites. Order more than you think.

  • Mussels

    A bowl for the middle of the table, with bread for the juice.

Good to know

Before you come
  • Price guide Around KES 1,300–3,200 per person, depending on what's ordered.
  • Groups and celebrations Birthdays, family lunches, weddings and meetings — call ahead and we'll set the room.
  • Garden seating Outdoor tables under the trees; ask for one when you book.
  • Takeaway and delivery Call the kitchen to order.
